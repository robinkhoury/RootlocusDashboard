You can also type G(s) straight into the formula bar at the top. Enter Im > 0 for a complex pair; its conjugate is added for you. You can also drag poles and zeros on the s-plane and watch the locus reshape. The loop is G(s) = K·N(s)/D(s) with unity negative feedback, so the closed-loop poles solve D(s) + K·N(s) = 0.

    A point s lies on the root locus only if the angle condition holds: Σ∠(s − zᵢ) − Σ∠(s − pⱼ) = ±180°(2k+1). The gain that puts a closed-loop pole there comes from the magnitude condition: K = Π|s − pⱼ| / Π|s − zᵢ|. Move the cursor over the s-plane to test any point; click to pin it (clicks near the locus snap onto it).
